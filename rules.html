WHAT IS THE RED VERSE?

The Red Verse is a fictional universe containing a collection of different characters, worlds, stories, histories, and realities, all interconnected into one universal story.

The Red Verse Encyclopedia serves as the central archive for the Red Verse and its ever- expanding collection of stories and lore.
